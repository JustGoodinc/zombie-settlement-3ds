# Survivor Specification

- Skills 1–10: Shooting, Melee, Medical, Mechanical, Farming, Guarding. XP comes from use (hits, treatments, daily jobs). A level costs 6 + 4×level XP.
- Traits: Steady Aim, Tough (120 HP), Green Thumb, Field Medic, Tinkerer, Watchful.
- Jobs: Idle, Guard, Medic, Mechanic, Farmer, Worker, assigned in Base → Jobs. Settlers walk to their job plot, play the Work/Guard animation, and sleep on bedrolls by the campfire at night (guards stay up).
- Injury: below 50 HP accuracy suffers; below 35 HP movement slows. HP regenerates slowly at the settlement; the infirmary heals faster.
- Infection: a bite (12%) gives Early infection. Each stage lasts 20 game hours: Early → Advanced → Critical → death, then the survivor turns. Hidden infections (gate arrivals) reveal themselves at Advanced or through screening. Treatment needs Infirmary Lv ≥ stage for good odds (the scanner counts as +1 level). Field antibiotics cure Early. A quarantined survivor who turns is put down safely. Otherwise a zombie spawns where they were (or, off-screen, may infect someone).
- Recruitment: gate strangers stay for a while. Options: Accept, Reject, Screen (1 medicine, reliable), Accept into Quarantine. Survivors rescued on expeditions are offered on the summary. The population cap is enforced.
- Death is permanent and saved immediately. The pack drops as a recoverable bag at the location.
